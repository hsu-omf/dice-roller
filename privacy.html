Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Dice Roller is designed to be a simple dice utility. The app does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or other personal information.

Local Data

Dice rolls, custom presets, preferences, and app settings are stored locally on your device. This local information is not directly accessible by the developer.

Analytics

Dice Roller uses Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage and improve the app. Firebase Analytics may collect app, device, session, and usage information, including an app-instance identifier used by Firebase to identify a unique app installation.

The app may log limited product usage events, such as completed rolls, the type and number of dice used, whether a modifier was used, premium customization screen views, and premium purchase or restore milestones.

Dice Roller does not log actual dice roll results, decision dice option text, custom preset names, Apple IDs, email addresses, names, precise location, receipts, transaction IDs, purchase tokens, or payment card details.

Purchases

The app may offer optional in-app purchases through Apple's App Store. Purchases are processed by Apple. Dice Roller does not receive or store your payment card details. The app may log limited purchase-related analytics events, such as whether a premium purchase or restore action was completed, for product analytics purposes.

Advertising and Tracking

Dice Roller does not use advertising networks, does not show ads, and does not use analytics data for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

Third-Party Services

Dice Roller uses Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, for limited app usage analytics. Firebase's data practices are described by Google in its Firebase and Google Analytics documentation.
